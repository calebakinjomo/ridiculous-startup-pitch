# Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second

A make.fun bounty entry: one sentence, no more than 200 characters, pitching a
startup idea so strange it sounds like a joke — yet specific enough that a
reader can see the customers and the money.

## The pitch

> We sell naming rights to potholes: local shops pay $49 a month to brand the crater outside their door, because every driver slows down, stares, and remembers the name.

**167 characters** (one sentence, within the 200-character limit).

## Why it holds together

- **The odd thing being sold:** naming rights to potholes — a branded plaque (and
  a stencil) on the crater outside a shop.
- **Who pays:** local shops, $49 a month, for hyperlocal advertising.
- **Why it works:** a pothole is the one billboard every driver is forced to look
  at. They slow down, they stare, they remember the name — and a sponsored hole
  is a hole the neighbourhood is suddenly very keen to see fixed, which is good
  for the shop, the street, and the sponsor's name.
